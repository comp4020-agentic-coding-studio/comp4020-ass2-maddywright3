Groups of four, split into two teams of two. One team spends the first part
of the lab building a project (any small, quick build — a short procedure,
a simple diagram, a mini-spec) and then hands it over to the other team
using nothing but a slide deck, exactly as week 7's lecture describes NASA's
own handover process. No live explanation beyond what's on the slides.

## Before the lab

Read week 7's lecture — the Mars Climate Orbiter case is your model for
what "each side, correctly, believes they're done" looks like from the
inside.

## In the lab

The handing-over team builds for fifteen minutes, then presents their deck
without additional narration. The receiving team then has to actually use
or continue the work from the deck alone, and has to say out loud, in the
room, the exact moment they discover something the deck didn't cover.

## Submitting

A joint writeup, from all four: what the deck assumed the reader already
knew, and whether that assumption was reasonable or just familiar to the
person who wrote it. Due Friday 23 April, 5pm.

Groups of four (two handover pairs).
