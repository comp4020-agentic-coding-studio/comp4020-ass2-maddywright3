Groups of four, split into a founder pair and an investor pair. Founders
pitch a fictional startup with a stated traction number ("100 paying
users") that the investors have privately agreed, before the roleplay
starts, is not actually where the bar will stay.

## Before the lab

Read week 8's lecture and play with its moving-goalpost widget several
times through — notice what it feels like from the visitor's side before
you play the investor's side of this.

## In the lab

Founders pitch to the number they were told mattered. Investors, in
character, express real interest — and then raise the number, the same way
the widget's target jumps on approach. Run this for two or three rounds.
Founders: write down the moment you noticed the goal had moved.

## Submitting

A joint writeup: the founders' account of when they noticed, the
investors' account of why they moved the bar each time, and one paragraph
connecting both to why this week's mechanic couldn't have been a plain
cost-curve. Due Friday 30 April, 5pm.
