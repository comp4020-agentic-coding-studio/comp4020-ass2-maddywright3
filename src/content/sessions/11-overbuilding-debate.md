Groups of three per side, two sides per debate. Motion: "A maintainer
should always keep polishing a shipped feature." One team of three argues
for, one against, drawing on week 11's lecture and Richard Gabriel's "Rise
of Worse Is Better" essay.

## Before the lab

Read week 11's lecture and the Gabriel essay it links in full — this
debate doesn't work if the "for" side hasn't actually read the strongest
version of the counter-argument they're meant to be rebutting.

## In the lab

Standard debate format: opening statement from each side (three minutes),
rebuttal (two minutes each), closing statement (one minute each). The
closing statement is where the marks concentrate — each side has to name
the specific point where the polish curve turns, i.e. where they'd concede
the other side is right, rather than defending an absolute position to the
end.

## Submitting

Each team's prepared opening statement and a short note on what their
closing concession was. Due Friday 21 May, 5pm.
