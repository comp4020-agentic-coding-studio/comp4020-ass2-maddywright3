Solo. Write a single diary entry — 400 to 600 words, first person, present
tense — from an invented ABD (all-but-dissertation) student, set on the
exact day or week their dissertation work quietly stopped. Not a summary of
their situation: the actual entry, in their voice, on that day.

## Before the lab

Read week 3's lecture and the Council of Graduate Schools material it
links. Come with a rough shape for your character: field, year, what
they've already finished, what's left.

## In the lab

Write the draft in the room. This is deliberately timed and unplanned —
the lab's own argument is that stalling rarely announces itself in advance,
so writing it cold is closer to the real thing than writing it from a neat
outline. Fifteen minutes of quiet writing, then read a paragraph aloud to
whoever's next to you and take two minutes of their reaction before you
finish the entry.

## Submitting

The finished entry, individually, with a one-line footer stating what
external event (if any) would need to happen for this character to write
tomorrow's entry — without saying whether they do. Due Friday 12 March,
5pm.

This one's solo — it's a personal voice, and a group diary entry reads like
neither person meant it.
