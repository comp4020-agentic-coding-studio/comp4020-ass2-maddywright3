Groups of three. Pick a real, visitable venue — a cafe, a library, a
student union space, anything with a public front and a less-public back —
and produce a short zine (four to eight pages, any medium: paper, PDF,
whatever your group can actually fold) auditing where its polish runs out.

## Before the lab

Read week 4's lecture. Visit your chosen venue, or draw on a recent visit,
before the lab — you need real observations, not invented ones.

## In the lab

Compare notes as a group and pick the three strongest examples of a
last-mile failure point that a customer notices but nobody budgeted to
fix — the survey week 4's lecture cites is your model for what counts as
"hidden": something that drives customers away silently, without a
complaint ever being filed. Storyboard the zine's pages together before
anyone starts drawing or laying out text.

## Submitting

The finished zine, as a group, plus a one-paragraph note on which of your
three examples you think the venue's own management doesn't know about.
Due Friday 19 March, 5pm.

Groups of three for this one — enough hands to actually make a zine in the
time available, few enough that everyone did something.
