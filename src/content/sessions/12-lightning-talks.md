Solo, in the room, one at a time. Before the lab, take week 12's reflective
quiz on the lecture page and note which domain (or domains) it flagged as
the one you've personally hit. In the lab, you get sixty seconds — timed,
not a suggestion — to tell the room about one real unfinished thing of
yours and which of the eleven earlier weeks' arguments actually explains
why it stalled.

## Before the lab

Read week 12's lecture and take its quiz. Pick the one real unfinished
thing you're willing to talk about for sixty seconds in front of the class
— it doesn't have to be dramatic, it has to be true.

## In the lab

Sixty seconds each, in a fixed order, no slides. The point of the strict
limit is the same point week 8's moving-goalpost widget makes about
targets: a talk with no limit quietly becomes a talk about everything,
which is a last-mile failure in miniature, live, in the room.

## Submitting

A one-paragraph written version of your talk, individually, naming the
domain you mapped it to. Due Friday 28 May, 5pm — the same day the final
project is due, since this is the last lab of the semester.

Solo — sixty seconds is a personal account, not a group one.
