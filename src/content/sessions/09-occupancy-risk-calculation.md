Pairs. You're given a fictional building at "98% complete" with a punch
list of outstanding items across the categories week 9's lecture and its
linked Procore reference describe (life-safety, code-compliance,
cosmetic). The job is to estimate, per category, how much occupancy risk
remains — not a single blended percentage, since the lecture's whole point
is that a building department doesn't grade on a slope.

## Before the lab

Read week 9's lecture and the Procore reference it links before the lab —
this lab is graded on using their categories, not inventing your own.

## In the lab

As a pair, sort the supplied punch list into the real categories, estimate
which items block occupancy outright versus which are cosmetic, and
identify the single highest-risk open item — the one that, if
unresolved, keeps the certificate from being issued regardless of how
finished everything else is.

## Submitting

The sorted punch list, the risk estimate per category, and the named
highest-risk item, from the pair. Due Friday 7 May, 5pm.

Pairs of two.
