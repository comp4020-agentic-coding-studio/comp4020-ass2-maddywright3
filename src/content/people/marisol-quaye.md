I built this course because I kept watching sharp people ship genuinely good
work and then never actually finish it — a demo that never became a
product, a thesis missing its conclusion, a restaurant that nails the food
and ignores the bathroom. Including, more than once, me. Twelve weeks,
twelve domains, one argument: finishing is a different skill from building,
and it's the one almost everyone is worse at.

## What comes to me

Anything about the course's shape — a week's argument, a weighting, an
extension decision above a tutor's discretion — comes to me directly.
Anything about a specific lab or Assignment 1 mark should go to Idris or
Priya first; I only see those if they've already been escalated.

## Consultation

Email for anything that needs a decision before the next session — I read
email daily during teaching weeks. For feedback on your own work, use the
weekly session instead: it's faster for you, and it's the only place I can
actually see what you're stuck on rather than guess at it from a written
question.
