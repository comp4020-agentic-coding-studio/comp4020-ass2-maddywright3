I spend most of my working life on the boundary this week's lecture is
about: the point where one team's finished, verified, "done" work gets
handed to another team who is about to build on top of it without being
able to see how it was made. I'm not here to relitigate Mars Climate
Orbiter — Marisol's lecture already does that — I'm here to talk about what
it's actually like to be the engineer on either side of a handover like
that, and why "write it down properly" is much harder advice to follow than
it sounds.

I join the week 7 session only; for anything about marking, extensions, or
the rest of the course, talk to Marisol or the tutors as usual.
