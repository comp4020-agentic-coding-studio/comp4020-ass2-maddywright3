I mark roughly half of each week's labs and split Final Project marking
with Idris. One tutor grading twelve labs and two written assessments alone
is exactly the kind of thing this course would tell you not to do — the
marking backlog is its own last mile, and it gets expensive fast if you
pretend one person can absorb it without anything slipping.
